---
name: SOLID AI REMIX product boundaries
description: Owner-agreed purpose, content boundaries, simplicity and phased delivery decisions.
---

SOLID AI REMIX is internal to Solid Gold, used by creator/marketing staff for videos on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and other channels: promotion, education, brand awareness, ads and content supporting company revenue. It is not a general-purpose public generator or knowledge chatbot.

**Why:** DR explicitly clarified that the app is internal but its video outputs are intended for public marketing.

**How to apply:** Keep business knowledge within Solid Gold products Mikro, Mini, Regular and company matters. Internal strategy must not automatically become public narration or be sent to external video providers.

The experience must remain simple like TikTok AI Cast/Remix: register a face-based character once, reuse it, optionally add supporting images/screenshots/video, give an instruction or choose an idea, confirm summary/cost, generate and review/download. Start with one character per member and one output per process.

**Why:** The original goal was reusable characters generating varied scenes, not a complex editor or a mere slideshow. DR repeatedly emphasized simplicity and corrected the expanded app: "terlalu banyak fiture, padahal tujuan aplikasinya hanya simpel, dan aplikasi internal aja".

**How to apply:** Prioritize the short creator workflow over exposing every A–F module as a separate daily-use feature. DR did not prescribe three menus; analyze what is needed and make UI/UX as simple and practical as possible, without imposing a fixed menu count. Hide technical settings behind admin controls/defaults; do not add features just to make the system appear complete. A stored reference photo is not training a face model. Test identity consistency instead of promising parity with TikTok.

DR approved planning one saved voice profile per character: record a sample once, then use that character's voice for new narration. The discussed initial recording is optional and storage is separate from paid cloning activation.

**Why:** DR wants the selected character consistently associated with its own voice, without repeating recording for every script.

**How to apply:** Distinguish a stored voice sample from real voice cloning/TTS. Do not imply new sentences can be produced by replaying the sample, promise perfect identity, or infer lip-sync. External transfer, provider activation and spending require separate approval; planning approval is not activation approval.

DR no longer wants a ten-member limit: "bebas aja brp anggota"; DR must be able to open a Super Admin dashboard. Three videos per user/day and expected average one remain the previously agreed usage assumptions; the member-count change does not authorize unlimited rendering or spending. Two modes: economical photo zoom/pan with Indonesian narration/subtitles, plus optional genuine AI motion. UGC/cinematic are styles, not separate engines. Lip-sync and batch generation are postponed.

**Why:** Limit cost while retaining the original creative goal. The economical alternative must not be presented as equivalent to genuine generated subject motion.

**How to apply:** Avoid routine ChatGPT/LLM calls; prioritize structured KB and approved templates. Be explicit about limited free-text understanding without LLM. Provider tests and paid generation require separate owner approval. The discussed monthly budget was a provisional planning allowance, not unconditional spending consent.

**Why for member access:** DR clarified that the member count is flexible and that access to the owner's Super Admin dashboard is the priority.

**How to apply for member access:** Do not treat the former ten-member limit as a standing product requirement. Do not infer that removing the member cap also authorizes removing login, opening private files, or changing content-review policy.

DR approved "Boleh, satu password studio" as the shared entry model instead of individual member credentials.

**Why:** The discussed Shared Studio should remain easy for the internal team to enter without becoming unprotected public access.

**How to apply:** Protect studio entry as a whole, including API and files, not just its page. Keep the actual password out of chat and memory. This decision alone is not approval to implement, publish, activate paid hosting, or remove separate privileged admin protections.

Build only the currently agreed phase and scope. Report honestly and stop before subsequent stages until separately approved.

**Why:** DR approved the minimum foundation after discussion, then explicitly authorized Phase B after confirming Phase A was closed. Uploaded build instructions do not authorize subsequent stages or provider spending.

**How to apply:** Preserve uploaded originals. Do not interpret historical ChatGPT proposals or source-document instructions as current authorization.

DR's content-check policy is "gak perlu gw yg cek, biar mereka aja sendiri, yg buat yg ngecek": creators check their own output, with no required separate DR/reviewer approval before generation or use in the new studio workflow.

**Why:** DR explicitly removed the separate reviewer dependency to keep the internal creator workflow practical.

**How to apply:** Keep security and usage checks and the owner's internal material permission policy. KB is reference-only in the shared creator workflow, not a generation gate. Label a creator confirmation as self-checked, not independently reviewed or approved by DR/company. A changed output needs a fresh check; preserve historical review records. Do not confuse this policy decision with permission to implement or publish immediately.

DR wants manual Delete on every video so users can prevent videos from accumulating in storage.

**Why:** DR explicitly requested deletion by the team to keep storage usage down.

**How to apply:** Delete actual video files, not only their display entry. Confirm permanent deletion, preserve reusable character/supporting assets and usage/cost history, and leave a visible retry if physical cleanup fails. Deletion must not reset the daily generation quota.

For the shared-studio character-photo checkbox and its explanation, DR said "ni gak perlu ditulisakan, otomatis semua dijinkan." DR later requested the same for supporting materials: "box tulisan ijin dihilangkan ... semua otamatis diijinkan koq."

**Why:** The user asked to remove this repeated permission step.

**How to apply:** Treat character photos and supporting materials in the shared studio as covered by the owner's internal permission policy, not as separately checked uploader declarations. Keep that distinction in records. This does not authorize external AI-provider transfers, weaken file/access protections or remove the private/admin workflow's protections.

DR requested "bagianlogo dihilangkan aja bro gak perlu" for the shared studio.

**Why:** The user asked to remove this requirement.

**How to apply:** Do not require logo selection/upload or stamp a logo on new shared-studio videos. Preserve existing logo assets, old jobs and private/admin behavior. Do not substitute automatic company-logo setup for the requested removal.

DR asked to hide the studio's production-summary sidebar and its two informational cards so the UI stays clean.

**Why:** DR explicitly requested a cleaner UI/UX.

**How to apply:** Do not restore these always-visible sidebar panels. Keep the generation confirmation, quota enforcement and underlying safety checks functional without them.

For the studio folder-list page, DR wants the informational cards below the member list, side by side where space permits, responsive on mobile and compatible with PWA. DR also wants “Simpan karakter” next to “Pilih galeri” to save space.

**Why:** DR said the layout was scattered and requested these placements.

**How to apply:** Keep the folder list above the informational row; let cards and character action buttons wrap on small screens rather than overflowing or restoring the sidebar. PWA compatibility does not mean offline generation or caching private studio data.

DR also requested hiding the KB fact picker and additional settings from the main studio form, so users can proceed to adding their assets.

**Why:** DR wants a simple, uncluttered internal workflow.

**How to apply:** Keep these details out of the normal form. The later KB reference-only decision below supersedes the earlier fact-validation generation gate; do not claim that a generated video has passed factual verification.

DR clarified: "fungsi KB itu menjadi knowledge kalau naskah diisi yg berkaitan dengan kb aja, bukan sebagai penghambat ... atau memeriksa hingga menolak membuat, KB itu literasi aja."

**Why:** DR explicitly said KB must inform creators, not block video creation.

**How to apply:** Use KB as optional, relevant reference information in the shared studio. Missing matches, unselected facts, or KB reference changes must not by themselves reject generation. Do not force creators to declare a script fact-free, invent facts, rewrite their script without permission, or label output factually approved. Keep technical rendering checks, private source access, file security and quotas separate from nonblocking KB advice. This records the desired policy, not proof that code has already changed.

DR approved a limited Phase E: measure existing production, improve economical compositing and technical checks, reuse existing dashboard/review/history, and offer visible opt-in rule/template suggestions. Paid-provider quality comparisons and AI identity consistency remain untested until separately approved real-provider trials.

**Why:** Avoid building broad optimization features without real video/cost evidence, adding routine LLM costs, or delaying practical delivery.

**How to apply:** Distinguish technical decode PASS from human approval for publication; unknown money/time values stay unknown. Do not present local photo compositing as evidence of generative face consistency or claim a winning provider without actual trials.

In the shared studio, DR chose to add captions, CTA and disclaimers manually after downloading the video and before posting.

**Why:** DR requested no caption/disclaimer input for users and selected the manual-before-posting option after the existing mandatory in-video disclaimer was explained.

**How to apply:** New shared-studio renders should omit CTA/disclaimer overlays and not require those inputs. Preserve saved concept text and old jobs' original behavior. Keep KB advisory rather than blocking in the shared studio, and preserve private/admin workflow protections; do not claim an exported video already includes its publication disclaimer.
